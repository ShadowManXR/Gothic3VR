# Gothic 3 VR 0.22.9 — configurable melee weight and stabilization

New live controls are in the existing weapon pages.1H Melee Weapon: Weight20%, Stabilization15ms, shared settings but independent filters for the two dual-wield hands.2H Melee: Two-hand weight30%, Two-hand stabilization20ms, active with actual support grip. Staff / Halberd: One-hand weight25%/stabilization15ms and Two-hand weight30%/stabilization20ms. Weight ranges0..200%, stabilization0..150ms, both in5-unit steps. Both values0 disables the profile exactly. Save settings applies live within the normal one-second settings refresh. Weight is a handling scale, not kilograms. Existing INIs load the light defaults without rewriting saved preferences.

The new MeleeHandling history lives in true XR metres/orientation. An identity GripWorld pose supplies the current world anchor, including centre rotation/position, native heading, scale and camera height. Helpers use the same OpenXR/Genome Z reflection as GameEyeTransform. Walking, body motion and native turning therefore do not become delayed weapon movement. Exponential first-order position/orientation response adds weight and reduces jitter without a spring/overshoot. Rotation lag is bounded to8+18*weightFactor degrees and translation lag to2+3.5*weightFactor cm. Profiles remain responsive and keep full reach after settling; transient lag of the hand/weapon assembly is intentional. Session/item changes, tracking gaps, clock reversal, large tracking jumps and reset events discard stale history. Live parameter changes and staff one/two-hand transitions retain continuous history; explicit0/0 returns direct tracking.

The filter acts on the final melee grip before IK. It moves the entire hand/weapon assembly, keeping the cached item-to-hand attachment fixed. A supported2H/staff has one final filtered frame: the same rigid delta transports its mesh, both palm orientations and the secondary wrist. Staff one-hand filtering occurs before support acquisition so the accepted capture-current-pose behavior is preserved. On staff release the one-hand profile continues from the supported frame. Repeated XR timestamps and the frozen stereo palette do not integrate the filter twice. No independent pair of filters fights over attached palms.

Physical contacts use the displayed weapon blade. For the new filtered profiles, swing orientation and hand travel also use the filtered tracked pose; damage/stamina cannot advance using an unweighted invisible orientation. Existing distance/speed thresholds, damage/skills/stamina calculations, power attacks, legacy unsupported2H heavy handling, and hit cooldowns are unchanged. Bows, crossbows, shield, empty hands and magic items retain their existing handling. The accepted progressive arm-twist solver remains and follows the weighted final grip. Native camera, controls, locomotion and game assets are untouched. Log vr_melee_handling identifies active hands/profiles.
