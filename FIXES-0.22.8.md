# Gothic 3 VR 0.22.8 — progressive wrist, forearm and upper-arm rotation

The tracked hand previously received the controller orientation, while upper/forearm IK aligned only the joint endpoints. Rotating the wrist therefore left most of the avatar's arm stationary. The requested behavior distributes rotation progressively through all three segments.

After the accepted positional/stretch IK, the new visual twist solver compares the final tracked hand with a bind-pose reference aligned to the solved forearm. Small axial rotations stay at the wrist. Above 12 degrees the forearm gradually contributes; the wrist approaches a soft 22-degree limit. Forearm contribution is unrestricted up to 60 degrees and approaches a soft 85-degree relative limit, with the remainder carried by the upper arm. Soft knees avoid snaps at transitions. These are approximate visual joint limits, rather than anatomical measurements or a body-tracking system.

The forearm receives the combined forearm/upper contribution and the upper arm receives its own contribution about the respective solved bone axes. Longitudinal projection removes IK stretch from the twist measurement. Bind-pose references compensate native animation roll instead of interpreting it as controller motion. Wrist bending remains in the final hand pose; the twist layer does not swing a bone away from its solved endpoints. The shoulder, elbow and wrist positions remain those of the existing IK. Full controller reach, hand calibration and exact target hand orientation are retained. This is axial redistribution, not a new elbow-position or camera solver.

Each arm has independent angle-unwrapping state, preventing partial joint distributions from jumping when the orientation crosses +/-180 degrees. Undefined twist at a perpendicular half-turn retains the last valid sample. Actor/session changes, missing tracked input, time reversal, tracking gaps and rig/reset events clear the state. No time-based smoothing or controller input delay is introduced. Supported two-hand poses use the same final rendered grip orientation; frozen stereo palettes reuse one solved chain for both eyes.

The change affects only upper-arm/forearm render deltas. The final hand/finger palette, all weapon attachment and combat-sample logic, accepted2H grab pose, physical reach, bow/crossbow aim/nock handling, native skills, stamina, controls and camera remain. The0.22.7 loaded/static and launched projectile visibility fix is retained byte-for-byte in its ranged modules. Native actors and shared resources are not modified; the existing per-eye palette restoration still owns all writes.
