# Dual wield — 0.14.0

The user confirmed the 0.14.0 headset probe: both dual wield 1H weapons remain visible and physical combat works with both hands. This records the tested setup; it does not validate every weapon template or physical parrying.

Two distinct drawn native 1H weapons (use type 2) follow their respective calibrated Quest controller. The shared 1H Melee Weapon pitch/yaw/roll profile applies to each native attachment; Left hand / Right hand device offsets remain independent. Native dual wield skills, inventory slots, draw/sheath and weapon requirements are unchanged.

The hash-verified Game.dll GetWeapon(player, true/false) implementation selects held RightHand (1) / LeftHand (2) inventory slots. It does not select equipped/back slots. No fallback spawns, copies, equips or recreates a missing weapon.

For both 1H entities, the renderer snapshots each native hand-relative attachment before moving anything, prepares its exact tracked spatial transform before eye frustum collection, and uses that same attachment/pose for the hero palette and rendered blade sample. A native hidden rendering flag is borrowed as visible only during stereo rendering of the drawn tracked entity. Original local matrices and visibility flags are restored after each eye, at frame end and on faults. Before repeated hero palette updates, non-1H transforms retain their original restoration path while prepared 1H spatial poses stay in place. This avoids applying the tracked hand delta twice or culling the left sword at its old animation position.

Both hands publish separate blade/contact samples. NativeMelee keeps independent CombatChannel state: consumed timestamp, equipment/session identity, Swing, intensity and target hit set. Right motion never creates left energy; left tracking loss/equipment changes do not erase right strokes. Simultaneous tracking samples are processed once per hand on the application thread. Each contact uses its actual owned weapon's Damage property and inflictor; the existing native post-armor/scaled HP cap remains in effect. Both weapons can hit the same target once in their own strokes. Weapon collisions with the user's other weapon are excluded through the existing ownership walk. Physical parrying and native attack animations are not added.

47 tests pass. The dual wield regression exercises different native attachment orientations, left/right pose independence, matching pre-cull/palette transforms, locomotion translation, independent strokes and same-target hits, per-weapon caps, duplicate eyes, tracking loss, equipment replacement and menu/stale sample reset. Existing damage ABI, geometry, staff, 2H, bow and real GPU/native binary tests remain included. These tests do not prove live engine culling or headset combat acceptance.
